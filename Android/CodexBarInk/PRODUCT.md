# CodexBar Ink

## Register

Product. This is a working companion dashboard for BOOX e-ink readers, not a marketing surface.

## Users and purpose

CodexBar users keep the reader visible beside their computer to glance at current Codex provider quotas,
daily cost, weather, and a focus timer without opening the Mac app.

The primary job is to make changing operational data legible at a glance on a color e-ink display.

## Personality

Restrained, precise, calm, and utilitarian. It should feel like a well-typeset instrument panel: generous
enough to scan, dense enough to remain useful, and free of ornamental chrome.

## Anti-references

- Colorful dashboard cards, gradients, shadows, and decorative illustration.
- Low-contrast small text or subtle gray-on-gray hierarchy.
- Dense borders, repeated rules, and visual noise.
- Continuous animation or unnecessary full-screen refreshes.
- Touch interactions that can stop in ambiguous intermediate states.

## Accessibility and e-ink constraints

- Use true black for primary content and strong neutral gray for secondary copy.
- Keep essential labels and metadata readable at normal BOOX viewing distance.
- Prefer stable geometry, clear 48dp-or-larger controls, and partial regional refreshes.
- Preserve system-language localization and support portrait plus both landscape orientations.
- Use text and native drawing for live data; do not depend on image snapshots for charts.
